# Echoes of Time (Blender 3D Scene & Animation)

A 3D scene and short animation built in Blender: modelling, texturing, lighting, and rendering a complete environment (a textured brick house with surrounding trees and terrain) plus an animated sequence.

## Skills covered

- 3D modelling (buildings, props, organic shapes like trees)
- UV unwrapping and texturing (brick, wood, roof tiles, foliage)
- Lighting and camera setup for rendering
- Animation and rendering a final sequence
